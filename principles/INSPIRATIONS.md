# INSPIRATIONS — where to look, and how hard to trust it

The inspiration layer of the suite: where reference material comes from for DESIGN-mode rounds (§B) and taste-overlay growth (§G). Method distilled from Chase AI [7FU98O0JLHs]; source tiers corrected by field observation.

**The founding correction:** Chase's method — curate a library, screenshot + save links, cluster into aesthetic families, feed references into prompting — is the right method and this suite is built on it. His named venues (Dribbble Popular, Pinterest) are where field data disagrees: a survey of ~40 Dribbble-Popular dashboard shots found the popular corpus **is the mean itself** — near-identical layouts, flawless fake data, no empty or error states [field 2026-07-24, n≈40]. Popularity on a portfolio site optimizes for a beautiful 400px thumbnail, not for a person deciding what to do at 6am. So: **his method, better venues.** Real and inspectable outranks fashionable and flat.

## Tier A — real shipped sites, full source inspectable

Galleries that link to **live websites** — open any entry, and devtools gives you the entire implementation: real markup, real states, real content surviving real constraints. This is the "Dribbble but view full source" tier.

| Source | What it curates | Why it's tier A |
|---|---|---|
| godly.website | "astronomically good" live sites | links out to the real thing; strong on restraint |
| land-book.com | live landing pages, product sites | large, tagged, real |
| siteinspire.com | live sites, editorial taste, style/type/subject filters | oldest serious curation; filters map well to the ladder's materials |
| minimal.gallery | live minimal sites | antidote to card-soup maximalism |
| httpster.net | live sites, brutalist/typographic lean | breaks the SaaS mean hardest |

**Method on this tier [P06RgnUKX_I]:** don't stop at the screenshot — grab the *section* from the live site and study its structure (the Paper Snapshot practice: live sections as editable material, not pictures). A saved link beats a saved image; a saved link with a one-line note in your own words about *why* beats both — the note is what makes it taste rather than collection (§G).

The deepest tier: not just what it looks like, but *why*, with production code attached.

## Usage rules

1. **Steal structure, not paint.** Genre and information architecture are the ladder's upper rounds; palette is round 4. Copying a tier-E surface's colors imports its mean; understanding a tier-A site's sectioning imports its thinking.
2. **Every saved reference carries: link (not just screenshot) · author/URL credit · one line in your own words on why.** Uncredited saves rot into "mine"; why-less saves rot into hoarding [§G].
3. **Cluster into named families** (Chase's five-aesthetic-families move [7FU98O0JLHs]) — the clusters become B3 paradigm/material candidates.
4. **Inspiration ≠ taste.** The library is raw material; the taste overlay grows only from verdicts on *rendered* work (§G). A folder of admired screenshots is a hypothesis about taste, exactly like an agent-curated overlay.
5. **Tier gradient = trust gradient.** A pattern seen in GOV.UK research outranks the same pattern on Mobbin, which outranks it on Dribbble. When tiers disagree, the lower-numbered tier wins.
